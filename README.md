## Hi there,  I'm Anastasia 👋

I'm an aspiring Data Analyst transitioning from linguistics and language education.

My journey into data analytics is a natural extension of my experience working with systems and communication. As a linguist and educator, I spent my days structuring information, identifying patterns, and evaluating performance metrics. Transitioning into data analytics has allowed me to combine these skills with modern tools (Python, SQL, A/B testing). Thanks to my teaching background, I am not only able to uncover insights in data, but also to effectively communicate them to the team in a clear and actionable way.

I am currently expanding my expertise in data analytics. Among other resources, I follow the Reveal the Data project (https://revealthedata.com/), which offers great insights into data visualization and BI systems, as well as the Trisigma Telegram community (https://t.me/trisigma_avito), where practical limitations and approaches to A/B testing are frequently discussed alongside links to relevant industry articles.

Let’s connect on Telegram: https://t.me/Graaben

- **Analytics:** A/B Testing, Statistical Analysis (t-tests, Mann-Whitney U, Z-test), Cohort Analysis, Retention & LTV Metrics.
